Chapter 01 — The reason

Cooking isn't the hard part. Deciding what to cook is.

If you're living alone, sharing a hostel room, or just started cooking for yourself, you've probably had the thought: you know you should eat better, but you don't have the time, skills or money for it.

Bachelor's Kitchen is built on one idea — eating high-protein, nutritious meals doesn't need supplements, fancy ingredients or hours in the kitchen. It just needs the right recipes: ones that use what your local kirana store already stocks, cook on the equipment you already own, and are priced for a real student or working bachelor's budget.
